# `zaino-header-chain`: First release

First release. The Validated Header tree, in memory: `HeaderChain::new(depth)` then `anchor(header, height)` (a trusted validator's header at its tip − depth = the final tip; no history from genesis, no store); `insert(header)` attaches a trusted header on its parent link and nBits work alone (its validator ran the consensus rules), keeps a bounded tree of branches above the final boundary and tracks the most-work tip; `vouch(block)` records a trusted validator's best chain had it, `finalizable()` = min(highest vouched, best − depth), `finalize` keeps the newest max(2 · depth, the difficulty context) final headers. With no vouch that deep, `peer_finalizable()` = best − depth unless an unfinal best-chain block holds under half the work of the highest vouched block (a difficulty collapse); `finalize_by_peers` moves final there on the caller's peer evidence, and `best_height_of(hash)` places a hash on the best branch. `VerifiedChain` is an immutable snapshot (`best`, `final_tip`, `hash_at`, `header_at`, `on_best`, `holds`, `locator`, `forks`, `branch`); at or below the final tip past the headers kept = final by definition. `validate(params, header, height, ancestors, now)` keeps every consensus rule (Equihash (200, 9), hash ≤ target, difficulty adjustment with ZIP 218's window, median time past, 2-hour future bound; `Rejected::ShortContext` when fewer ancestors are held than the rules need). `insert_untrusted(header, &params, now_unix)` attaches a peer's header only when every rule holds against the chain's own ancestors (`Params::for_network(network, &schedule)`). Feature `testing`: `HeaderViews` on a `MockChain` (anchored at genesis).
