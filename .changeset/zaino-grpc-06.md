# `zaino-grpc`: `GetSubtreeRoots`: an unknown `shieldedProtocol` is `UNIMPLEMENTED` (was `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, a shape the …

`GetSubtreeRoots`: an unknown `shieldedProtocol` is `UNIMPLEMENTED` (was `INVALID_ARGUMENT`, a shape the Android SDK does not survive with fast sync), and a `startIndex` or `maxEntries` above 65,535 is an empty or whole `OK` stream (was `INVALID_ARGUMENT`). The answer is whole or refused before its first byte.
