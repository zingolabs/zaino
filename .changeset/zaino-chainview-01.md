# `zaino-chainview`: First release

First release. One view over N trusted validators (`ChainView`, `ChainViewSubscriber`, one `EndpointPoller` each over `zaino_source::ChainDataSource`): `HeaderSync` fetches every validator's headers into a `zaino-header-chain` and publishes the most-work `VerifiedChain`; the served tip is `ChainTip { block, held_by }`, failing closed with `Unserved` when no trusted validator holds it, and finality moves only past a vouched block or, with no trusted validator vouching that deep, past best − depth once live peers in more than 5 netgroups carry it and no difficulty collapsed. A mempool transaction is servable once any validator lists it or a peer quorum admits it (below), with its fee, its spread across validators and peers, and a shared per-tip `MempoolTail` feed for `GetMempoolStream`. `submit` prechecks a transaction against the upgrade schedule `ChainView::new` takes and sends it to one random entry per attempt, resubmitting until it is seen spreading (`SubmitPolicy`). Peers join through `ValidatorP2pSource` (`with_peers`): `PeerWatch` folds its block `inv`s (`Heard { peer, hash }`), `PeerMempool` its per-peer mempool listings. A published `ChainViewSnapshot` names the `VerifiedChain` it was judged under (`chain`) and the transactions turned servable since another (`arrivals`); `ChainViewSubscriber::subscribe_published` wakes on every publish. Telemetry: `describe_metrics` gauges and `Alarms` (stale, ending, partitioned, eclipsed, finality paused). Feature `testing`: `ChainView::set_verified`, `ChainViewSnapshot::fixed`.
